# Video Assets

Place your own media in these folders:

- `characters/` — Wojak-style character PNGs and other recurring characters.
- `backgrounds/` — bedrooms, computers, offices, charts, gaming screens, dreamcore environments, etc.
- `props/` — phones, energy drinks, keyboards, signs, money, and other transparent PNG overlays.
- `sounds/` — user-supplied sound effects and other audio.
- `music/` — user-supplied background music.

## Audio / dialogue policy

The renderer does **not** require dialogue, voiceover, or generated speech. Videos should work as silent visual edits with on-screen captions. You can optionally supply your own audio later; the renderer should treat audio as an optional layer rather than a required input.

## Character style separation

Keep Wojak/shitpost assets separate from dreamcore assets. A dreamcore wizard scene should not automatically receive Wojak characters, and Wojak scenes should not automatically receive the wizard.

Use assets you have the right to use, especially for anything published commercially.
